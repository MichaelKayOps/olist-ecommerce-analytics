# Business Questions

## Project Objective

To analyse Olist’s e-commerce data to understand how sales performance, delivery reliability, and customer purchasing behaviour relate to business performance, identifying opportunities to improve revenue quality, delivery reliability, and customer retention.

## Intended Audience

This analysis is intended to support commercial, logistics, customer experience, and leadership teams in evaluating business performance, identifying operational weaknesses, and prioritising improvement opportunities.

## 1. Sales Performance

- How have revenue and order volume changed over time?
- Which product categories contribute most to revenue and order volume?
- How does average order value vary across product categories and states?
- Which states contribute most to revenue, and how concentrated is revenue across regions?

## 2. Delivery Performance

- What proportion of delivered orders arrived on or before the estimated delivery date?
- How do late-delivery rates and average delivery delays vary over time and across customer states?
- Is there an observed relationship between delivery delays and customer review scores?
- Where should delivery performance be investigated further?

## 3. Customer Behaviour and Satisfaction

- What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?
- How does repeat purchasing vary across 3-, 6-, and 12-month observation windows?
- How do review scores relate to repeat purchasing?
- How does repeat purchasing vary across customer groups?

## Intended Outcome

Provide evidence-based insights to help Olist protect its core revenue markets, improve delivery reliability in underperforming regions, and identify opportunities to increase customer lifetime value through better customer retention.
